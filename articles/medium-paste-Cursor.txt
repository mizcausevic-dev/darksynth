Darksynth is a note-only MIDI construction kit

Four kits, mechanical pulse, and a lead the return restates. The preview is an excerpt, not a master

Tags: midi, darksynth, electronic, music production, composition
The pack is four coordinated kits in 4/4. Tempos are 96, 104, 112, 120 BPM, one fixed integer per kit, inside the 90 to 125 range used for this project. Each kit has six core parts: drums, percussion, bass, rhythmic synth, lead, and pad. Every core part has two variations. That is 48 files. There is no reference-only count. Part files are 8 bars. Each kit also has one 32-bar arrangement.
The kits are Iron Gate in D Mixolydian, Black Motor in A major, Voltage Veil in G Lydian, Red Terminal in F major. k01.lead.a opens F#5 to A5, a minor third. k02.lead.a opens C#5 to A4, a major third. k03.lead.a opens B4 to E5, a perfect fourth. k04.lead.a opens F5 to G5, a major second. The lead hooks are different melodies. They are not transpositions of one line with the same velocities. Variation B of each part changes start times, pitches, and durations. Velocity alone is not the difference.
Each arrangement has four 8-bar sections. Bars 1-8 state the theme: drums, percussion, bass, lead, and pad, with the rhythmic synth silent. Bars 9-16 are the pulse section: drums, percussion, bass, rhythmic synth, and pad. The theme lead does not play in that section. Lead B is an alternate file and is not placed in the arrangement. Bars 17-24 are the open section: rhythmic synth B and pad B only. Bars 25-32 are the return. The return places the same lead A part as the theme, with the same drums, percussion, bass, and pad. The lead is restated. It is not a new tune.

k01.bass.a starts bar 1 D3, bar 3 C3, bar 5 G2, bar 7 A2. k02.bass.a starts bar 1 A2, bar 3 D3, bar 5 E3, bar 7 F#3. k03.bass.a starts bar 1 G2, bar 5 D3. k04.bass.a starts bar 1 Bb3, bar 3 F3, bar 5 D3, bar 7 C3.
Bass, rhythmic synth, and lead are monophonic. The pad may hold four notes. Drums and percussion are GM channel 10, MIDI channel index 9, with an explicit map: 36 kick, 37 side stick, 38 acoustic snare, 39 clap, 40 electric snare, 42 closed hat, 46 open hat, 49 crash. C4 is 60. The resolution is 960 ticks per quarter note. Offsets are 0. Seeds name the kits. Nothing is random. k01.lead.b G#4 at tick 11520 is outside D Mixolydian. k02.lead.b G4 at tick 15600 is outside A major. k03.lead.b C5 at tick 15360 is outside G Lydian. k04.bass.b F#3 at tick 27600 is outside F major. k04.lead.b F#4 at tick 15600 is outside F major.
No program changes are encoded. A conventional GM player will normally use its default piano program for pitched channels unless configured otherwise; compatible GM channel 10 percussion is treated separately. Player behavior may vary.
Darksynth is a creative label in this pack. It is not a claim of scene membership or a fixed genre boundary. Distortion and gain staging are not note-only MIDI properties. They are not in these files. The preview synth is a simple-synth with bounded drive: a short kick, noise for hats and snare, a dull saw for the bass, a triangle for the rhythmic synth, a pulse for the lead, and a slow tone for the pad. That mapping is documented. It is not a program change.

No human listening audition was performed for this build. The validator reopened the MIDI bytes and compared them with the canonical score. Lead intervals and departure flags were checked against those pitches. That is a structural check. The sample peak written beside the preview is the peak of the integer mix. It is not a loudness figure and it is not a true-peak reading. Nothing in this build is claimed as a master.
