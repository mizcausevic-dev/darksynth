The pack is four coordinated kits in 4/4. Tempos are 96, 104, 112, 120 BPM, one fixed integer per kit, inside the 90 to 125 range used for this project. Each kit has six core parts: drums, percussion, bass, rhythmic synth, lead, and pad. Every core part has two variations. That is 48 files. There is no reference-only count. Part files are 8 bars. Each kit also has one 32-bar arrangement.

The kits are Iron Gate in D Mixolydian, Black Motor in A major, Voltage Veil in G Lydian, Red Terminal in F major. k01.lead.a opens F#5 to A5, a minor third. k02.lead.a opens C#5 to A4, a major third. k03.lead.a opens B4 to E5, a perfect fourth. k04.lead.a opens F5 to G5, a major second. The lead hooks are different melodies. They are not transpositions of one line with the same velocities. Variation B of each part changes start times, pitches, and durations. Velocity alone is not the difference.

Each arrangement has four 8-bar sections. Bars 1-8 state the theme: drums, percussion, bass, lead, and pad, with the rhythmic synth silent. Bars 9-16 are the pulse section: drums, percussion, bass, rhythmic synth, and pad. The theme lead does not play in that section. Lead B is an alternate file and is not placed in the arrangement. Bars 17-24 are the open section: rhythmic synth B and pad B only. Bars 25-32 are the return. The return places the same lead A part as the theme, with the same drums, percussion, bass, and pad. The lead is restated. It is not a new tune.

k01.bass.a starts bar 1 D3, bar 3 C3, bar 5 G2, bar 7 A2. k02.bass.a starts bar 1 A2, bar 3 D3, bar 5 E3, bar 7 F#3. k03.bass.a starts bar 1 G2, bar 5 D3. k04.bass.a starts bar 1 Bb3, bar 3 F3, bar 5 D3, bar 7 C3.

Bass, rhythmic synth, and lead are monophonic. The pad may hold four notes. Drums and percussion are GM channel 10, MIDI channel index 9, with an explicit map: 36 kick, 37 side stick, 38 acoustic snare, 39 clap, 40 electric snare, 42 closed hat, 46 open hat, 49 crash.
