# Kit identity

Container lengths, tempos, and collections were fixed before note generation. No stochastic draws.

Every arrangement is theme, pulse, open, return. The open section is thinner and has no drums, percussion, bass, or lead. The return restates motif A. Motif and pulse do not share a section. A bar stays 3840 ticks.

## k01 Iron Gate

Iron Gate is D Mixolydian at 128. The keys hold one chord for two bars, then rest before the next. The motif leaves a rest on the downbeat, rises a minor third, and sits out two later bars. Drums keep the kick on beat 1 and the snare on beat 3. The pulse uses straight eighths for four bars and is silent after that.

- Keys A changes chord every two bars: D dominant seventh, C major seventh, G major seventh, A minor seventh.
- The open section is keys B, pulse B, and pad B. It has no drums, no bass, and no motif.
- The return places motif A again, with the same drums, bass, keys, and pad as the theme.

Limitations: Motif B is an alternate file. It is not placed in the arrangement, so it does not sit on the theme. The pulse and the motif are in different sections. Do not stack them.
